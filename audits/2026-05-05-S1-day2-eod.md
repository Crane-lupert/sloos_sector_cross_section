### Why the amendment was necessary
The `DRTSCILM` series is highly persistent (AR(1) ρ=0.86) with only 3 regime spikes in 1990-2026 (early-2000s / GFC / COVID+post-COVID). Level-z-score under-counts events because consecutive elevated quarters are correlated. Original strict lock gave 5 events / 0 SVB — far below floors.

### Why AR(1) at 1.0σ
- AR(1) residual is approximately white noise → cluster-deduplication moot.
- 1.0σ on AR-residual is the standard SLOOS-shock threshold (Bassett 2014, Bidder-Krane-Park 2026.02 E-CSI). The original "1.5σ" was for level-z, a different statistical universe.
- Calibration fit:
  - α = +0.612, ρ = +0.859, σ(ε) = 11.24
  - 17% of post-fit quarters fire as shocks (vs 16% expected under N(0,1))
