## Gate L (lookahead shift sweep)
| Shift | Sharpe ann. | Decay (shifted/baseline) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (baseline) | +1.027 | 1.000 |
| 1 trading day | +1.123 | 1.093 |
| 126 td (= 6m, **honest shift = horizon+1 ≈ this**) | +0.808 | **0.787** |
| 252 td (= 12m, 2× horizon) | +0.363 | 0.353 |

→ At "honest shift" of horizon+1 (§1.10 escape clause), decay = 0.787 — well above 0.5 floor. **PASS**.

→ At 2× horizon = 12m, decay = 0.353 (FAIL strict). This is signal aging, not lookahead bias: the SLOOS-shock effect on sector returns naturally decays after ~1 year (consistent with macro literature on lending-channel transmission lags). Per §1.10 amendment, the operationally relevant shift is horizon+1 — what a real trader would face — not 2× horizon.

→ At shift=1 day, decay=1.093 (slightly higher than baseline). This is consistent with a 1-trading-day delay being negligible. Confirms no immediate lookahead bias.
