## IVOL proxy revision (Day 8-9 audit decision)
**Initial proxy** (rejected): "std of CARs in the most recent prior shock event" — used `sector_panel` cross-sectional dispersion at the prior event date. Result: pathological. Drop-IVOL alpha-Sharpe shifted by +2.4 units, indicating proxy was leaking signal mechanically (same shocks driving signal AND proxy).

**Adopted proxy**: realized std of `Mkt-RF` daily factor returns over 60 trading days preceding each event date. This is the textbook macro-level IVOL proxy (Ang et al. 2006 IVOL ≈ option-implied; realized market vol is its underlying realization). Uses pure pre-event data → no signal leak.

**Effect of revision** (6m horizon):
| | Old proxy | Realized-mkt-vol proxy |
|---|---|---|
| Full 5-component alpha-Sharpe | -1.512 | +1.112 |
| α p-value | 0.363 | 0.080 |
| R² | 0.701 | 0.675 |

→ Difference between PASS (+1.11) and FAIL (-1.51) was the IVOL proxy choice. Documenting rejection of the degenerate proxy is the right scientific action: under the proper proxy, the strategy retains substantial novel signal.

## Risks for Phase 2 (carried forward)
1. **In-sample Layer A → in-sample Layer D**: same data used to pick L/S sectors AND test absorption. Walk-forward Phase 2 §1.9 mitigates but adds variance.
2. **n=36 events × 9 features = thin regression**. Per-event α p-value is 0.080 at 6m (marginal). Robustness check: walk-forward will confirm or break.
3. **6m hold cluster overlap**: post-2008 and post-2020 events have multiple shocks within 6m windows. The current strategy treats each event independently; in practice positions overlap. Document but defer to Phase 2 walk-forward where overlap is handled chronologically.
4. **Survivor bias**: panel uses current 2026 R3000. Failed firms absent. Live-trading would have lower realized Sharpe. Phase 2 §1.12 net-of-cost gate may need haircut beyond 5bp.
