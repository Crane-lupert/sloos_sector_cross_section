### Phase 1 risks to watch
1. **Sign asymmetry**: tightening vs easing shocks may have different directional patterns (preview: Healthcare 6m sees both signs negative; Energy 1m differs by 4× across signs). Layer A should report sign-stratified t-stats too, not just unsigned.
2. **Survivor bias**: panel uses current 2026 R3000 universe back-filled to 1990. Failed firms (delisted) are absent. For Phase 1 layer_a this is acceptable but Phase 1 layer_d (live-trading-tradability) needs to address. Document for §7 mechanism risk if Layer D Δ Sharpe is borderline.
3. **Cap-weighted vs equal-weighted market**: current panel uses equal-weighted cross-sectional mean as benchmark. Layer A may swap to cap-weighted for live-tradability robustness — Phase 1 EOD audit decision point.
4. **Healthcare 6m -523bp t=-11.94**: extreme magnitude. Confirm this isn't driven by 1-2 outlier firms (winsorize check in Phase 1).
